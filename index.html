Research paper

SafeLift: The Next-Generation Borewell Rescue Bot

Problem
Children trapped in borewells and other confined spaces face risks during rescue operations, including physical injury, limited oxygen supply, and delays in assessing their health. Conventional rescue methods may lack integrated oxygen delivery and continuous monitoring of vital signs, such as heart rate, body temperature, and oxygen saturation. SafeLift proposes an approach to improve child safety during emergency rescue operations.
Proposed system
SafeLift: The Next-Generation Borewell Rescue Bot is a proposed robotic rescue system designed to help extract children safely from borewells and other confined spaces. The concept combines a robotic arm and gripping mechanism with an oxygen delivery system, vital-sign monitoring sensors, camera-based observation, operator controls, and thermal sensing. Its intended goal is to support safer lifting, provide supplementary oxygen, and communicate health information to rescuers during an emergency. The document describes a proposed invention, not a verified deployed or clinically validated system.
Method
The proposed approach involves lowering a remotely operated robotic system into a confined shaft, locating the trapped child using camera and sensing components, and positioning a robotic arm or gripping mechanism to support extraction. The design incorporates a proposed oxygen delivery mechanism and sensors for monitoring heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature, and blood oxygen saturation. Camera feeds and sensor readings are intended to help rescuers observe the situation and make informed decisions. These methods are described as a design concept; the document does not establish that the complete system has been experimentally validated.
My contribution
Contributed to the SafeLift research and invention-disclosure project as a Computer Science Engineering student specializing in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Woxsen University. The project focused on developing the concept of a next-generation borewell rescue robot incorporating robotic lifting, oxygen support, vital-sign monitoring, and operator-controlled rescue functionality.
